\begin{block}{Failure Analysis: What the Errors Mean}
\centering
\minicard{failure}{0.21\linewidth}{Topic overlap}{same passage topic}
\hfill
\minicard{warmgold}{0.21\linewidth}{Granularity}{too broad / specific}
\hfill
\minicard{purple}{0.21\linewidth}{Label artifact}{alias or paraphrase}
\hfill
\minicard{deepteal}{0.21\linewidth}{Dilution}{answer hidden in text}

\vspace{0.22cm}
\begin{tabularx}{0.96\linewidth}{>{\bfseries}p{0.28\linewidth}X}
High-sim wrong & Often caused by topical relatedness, long passages, or semantically close but incorrect answers. \\
Low-sim correct & Usually caused by short-answer vs. long-context mismatch or extra generated context. \\
Label artifact & Automatic rules are stricter than human judgment for aliases and paraphrases. \\
\end{tabularx}
\end{block}
